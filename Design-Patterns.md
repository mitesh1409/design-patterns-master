# Design Patterns

There are different types of Design Patterns.

Lets understand this.

We face different kinds of problems while writing code.  
Programmers face the following recurring problems.  

1. How to create the objects?
2. How objects are related to each other? OR
   What will be the relationship between the objects? OR
   How do we structure this objects to form a bigger structure or a bigger module?
3. How objects will communicate with each other? OR
   Communication between objects OR
   How they will behave with each other?

Design Patterns solve this common problems.

## #1 Creational

**Problem**  
How to create the objects?

**Solution**  
Creational Design Patterns

Creational Design Patterns are:

* Prototype
* Singleton
* Factory
  * Abstract Factory
* Builder

All of these deal with creation of objects.

---

## #2 Structural

**Problem**  
How objects are related to each other? OR
Relationship between objects OR
What will be the relationship between the objects? OR
How do we structure this objects to form a bigger structure or a bigger module?

**Solution**  
Structural Design Patterns

Structural Design Patterns are:

* Facade
* Composite
* Adapter

OR

* Facade
* Proxy

---

## #3 Behavioral

**Problem**  
How objects will communicate with each other? OR
Communication between objects OR
How they will behave with each other?

**Solution**  
Behavioral Design Patterns

Behavioral Design Patterns are:  

* Command
* Strategy
* Observer

OR

* Iterator
* Observer
* Mediator
* State
